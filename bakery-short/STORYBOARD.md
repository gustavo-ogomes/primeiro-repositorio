# "The Last Cookie": 12s bakery short

**Format:** vertical 9:16, 12 seconds, 24 fps, about 1080x1920
**Style:** 3D animated cartoon characters (stylized, expressive, big readable faces) with photorealistic food. Warm, soft, golden key light.
**Palette:** cream (#F3EAD8) + electric royal blue (#1F3FFF), with marble white and chocolate browns as accents.
**Tone:** fast comedic timing. Hook, loss, despair, rescue, bliss.

---

## 1. Character and set locks (paste into every prompt)

**WOMAN:** a young woman with long, voluminous curly dark hair, small dark oval sunglasses, a cream chunky-knit cardigan, and an electric royal blue tote bag on her left shoulder. Stylized 3D cartoon proportions with a very expressive face.

**EMPLOYEE:** a cheerful young man with short dark hair, an electric royal blue baseball cap, and a cream apron. On the apron's chest is a small round badge showing a blue cookie with white dots. He has a friendly, proud smile.

**BAKERY:** a modern, cozy bakery with cream walls, electric royal blue shelves stocked with cookies and brownies, a white marble counter, a curved glass display case, and a sunny window with small marble tables. The light is warm and soft, like late morning.

**FOOD:** photorealistic. Thick gooey cookies with large chunks of chocolate bar and molten centers. Fudgy brownies with shiny, crackly tops.

**Negative / avoid:** text, letters, signage, brand logos, watermarks, subtitles, extra fingers, warped faces, changes to the characters' outfits, any sunglasses color other than dark, any blue other than electric royal blue.

> Note: the cookie badge is part of the costume, not a brand logo. If a generator removes it because the prompt says "no logos," replace "no logos" with "no brand logos, no lettering."

## 7. Edit notes

- Cut on action: shot 3's whip-pan should land exactly on her shocked face. Use one hard cut and no transitions.
- Shot 4 is the only "slow" beat. Keep it to 1.5s so the rescue in shot 5 hits fast.
- Color grade: lift the creams warm and keep the blues saturated but not neon. Cool shot 4 slightly, then warm back up in shot 5.
- Check character consistency across clips (sunglasses shape, tote color, badge on the apron) before the final cut.
